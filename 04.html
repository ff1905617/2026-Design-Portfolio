Skorochod Ukrainian Buffet

Year 2025

Role Brand Designer, Art Director

Skorochod Ukrainian Buffet is a family-friendly Ukrainian-American restaurant located in Chicago's Ukrainian Village. Food is a love language in the Slavic diaspora.

Skorochod's mission is to celebrate the fusion of American and Slavic culinary traditions by serving hearty, authentic and affordable food with plenty to go around!
