Home Sown Hydroponics

Year 2026

Role Designer, Engineer

Home Sown Hydroponics is a window-sill hydroponics kit that serves as an educational tool to help new gardeners get started growing food at home. The kit comes with a hydroponic system, a solar powered air pump, net pots and a series of seed packets.

The hydroponic system is modular. Each module has a male and a female thread at either end. They are made to be fastened together to make your system as long or as short as your window sill permits.
