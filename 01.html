Hellbrook Brewing Co.

Hellbrook Brewing Co. is a craft beer brewery based in Burlington, VT. It's named after the steepest, most treacherous trail up Mt. Mansfield, the highest peak in the state. Hellbrook is made for Adrenaline Junkies, Acid Burnouts and Backwoods Creeps — really anyone seeking thrills in the outdoors. Our brand essence is "summit freak" because Hellbrook is a "summit beer," a cool, refreshing reward meant to be enjoyed at the top of a mountain.
