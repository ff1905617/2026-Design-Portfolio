Spotify Scopes

Year 2026

Role UI/UX Designer, Developer

Spotify Scopes is a hypothetical feature for Spotify that allows users to create and share audio visualizers to play along with their favorite music. The feature centers around the "Creator Studio," where the user can adjust different parameters such as color, shape and beat sensitivity to create their own audio-visual experience.

Key Features

Create your audio visualizer.

Save and publish your visualizer.

Play your visualizer and screencast.

Web Audio API

To prototype the audio-reactive behavior behind Spotify Scopes, I built a functioning visualizer using the Web Audio API. It analyzes live audio in the browser and translates frequency and amplitude data into the shape, color and motion parameters explored in the Creator Studio. Interact with the live build below.

Research

User Personas

Harold, 29 — Electrician

Harold is a 29 year old electrician.
He works hard during the week and gets together with his friends on the weekend for wings and beer.
They take turns connecting Harold’s smart TV, playing music and music videos.
This weekend get-together has been a tradition for Harold and his friends for many years.

Pete, 24 — Underground DJ

Pete is a 24 year old night club bouncer and underground DJ.
He works from 5:00pm to 1:00am at the night club and carts his sound equipment to basement parties afterwards.
He is relatively new to DJ-ing and doesn’t have top of the line equipment.
He is interested in learning how to use generative art for visuals during his DJ sets.

Current Negative Flows

Harold

  1. Harold and his friends are watching music videos and socializing but are starting to get bored of watching the same videos.
  2. While they enjoy each other’s company, they want to find something new to do together.
  3. The group is already tired from the work week.
  4. Some of Harold’s friends start scrolling on their phones.

Pain Points

  • Harold and his friends are bored of watching the same videos.
  • They are already tired from the week and are starting to get sleepy.
  • They want something new to do together.
  • They start looking for mindless stimulation on their phones.

Pete

  1. Pete is looking for different ways to improve his DJ set in terms of visuals. He currently has an old strobe light and an overhead projector.
  2. He has been researching different ways to incorporate visuals into his set and has discovered generative art.
  3. In order to make generative art, he has to learn how to code. With his work schedule as a bouncer, it’s hard for him to find time to sit down and teach himself.
  4. Pete usually ends up sticking to what he already has and doesn’t branch out.

Pain Points

  • Pete’s equipment is limited.
  • He wants to learn how to make generative art but doesn’t know how to code.
  • Pete can’t spend money on expensive softwares or classes.
  • His sets are limited in terms of visuals.
